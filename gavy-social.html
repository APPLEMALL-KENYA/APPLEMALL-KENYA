Gavy Social.

A social media management product initiative focused on bringing content preparation, scheduling, publishing and engagement workflows into one operational workspace.

01 / WORKFLOW MODEL

From content creation to measurement

CONTENTComposer

Prepare post content and select publishing formats.

CHANNELSConnected accounts

Manage platform account connections and publishing destinations.

SCHEDULINGPublishing calendar

Plan scheduled posts and coordinate publishing times.

OPERATIONSPost management

Review scheduled, published and failed post states.

ENGAGEMENTComments & inbox

Bring customer engagement and social conversations into the workflow.

INSIGHTPerformance analytics

Provide visibility into publishing activity and channel performance.

02 / ENGINEERING CONSIDERATIONS

Integration reliability matters

Social publishing depends on external platform permissions, token lifecycle, API limits, content-format rules and asynchronous publishing outcomes. The product direction therefore emphasizes account connection management, scheduling state, failure visibility and operational review.

Status transparency: Gavy Social is presented as a product initiative and workflow development, not as a publicly released service with independently verified platform coverage or adoption metrics.
