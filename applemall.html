01 / PROBLEM & PRODUCT

Commerce is more than a checkout.

Applemall is designed around the operational work required to run a marketplace: catalogue administration, vendor onboarding, order handling, location-aware fulfilment, customer communication and performance reporting.

My contribution

As founder and product developer, I have shaped product workflows and worked on Django application features, integrations, deployment support and operational tooling. The case study describes product areas visible in the supplied application captures; it does not imply sole authorship of every component.

04 / ENGINEERING

Technical areas

  • Python and Django application development.
  • Relational data modelling and schema/migration maintenance.
  • Role-based operational workflows and form validation.
  • REST/API and third-party service integration.
  • Payment configuration and webhook-health visibility.
  • Deployment configuration and production troubleshooting.

Engineering considerations

  • Keep customer-facing checkout separate from privileged operations.
  • Represent order progress as traceable status transitions.
  • Associate delivery fees and fulfilment options with selected locations.
  • Provide role-specific tools for vendors, pickup agents and administrators.
  • Protect credentials and customer information in operational interfaces.
Evidence note: the screenshots shown in this case study are cropped to remove account details and browser chrome. Captures containing customer or vendor contact details, delivery addresses or credentials are excluded.
05 / EVIDENCE & ACCESS

Public product, private operations

The public storefront is available to explore. Administrative dashboards are not exposed to employers because they contain privileged controls and customer/vendor information. This case study documents the workflows through sanitized screenshots rather than providing admin credentials or access.
